In-Hand Salary Calculator (India)

Compare old vs new tax regime for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) and see your monthly take-home. Private: all maths runs in your browser.

How this is calculated

  • New regime: standard deduction ₹75,000; slabs 0–4L nil, 4–8L 5%, 8–12L 10%, 12–16L 15%, 16–20L 20%, 20–24L 25%, above 24L 30%; tax is nil up to ₹12L taxable income (with marginal relief just above it).
  • Old regime: standard deduction ₹50,000; slabs 0–2.5L nil, 2.5–5L 5%, 5–10L 20%, above 10L 30%; rebate up to ₹12,500 if taxable income ≤ ₹5L.
  • 4% health & education cess added. Surcharge is not included, so use this for incomes up to ₹50 lakh.
  • Estimate only, not tax advice. Check the Income Tax Department's official rules before filing.
