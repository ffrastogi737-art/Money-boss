FAQ

How is HRA exemption calculated?

It is the least of: (1) actual HRA received, (2) rent paid minus 10% of basic salary (+DA), (3) 50% of basic for Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai, or 40% elsewhere. The rest of the HRA is taxable.

Does HRA exemption apply in the new regime?

No. It is available only under the old tax regime.

Is rent paid to parents allowed?

Generally yes if it is real rent, with a rent agreement and payment proof, and the parent declares it as income. Check the rules or ask a tax professional. This is an estimate, not tax advice.

Do I need a landlord PAN?

Employers usually ask for the landlord's PAN when annual rent is above ₹1 lakh.
