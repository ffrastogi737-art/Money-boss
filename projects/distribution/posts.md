## 1. Reddit — reply to "old vs new regime?" threads (r/IndiaInvestments, r/personalfinanceindia)
Old vs new depends on how much you can actually deduct. Quick way to decide: compute tax both ways. New regime: ₹75k standard deduction, nil tax up to ₹12L taxable. Old regime: ₹50k standard deduction plus 80C (max ₹1.5L), HRA, etc. If your total old-regime deductions are small, new usually wins.
I got tired of doing this in a spreadsheet, so I built a free browser-only calculator (no signup, nothing uploaded; I'm the author): <salary tool link>. It shows monthly in-hand under both regimes. It ignores surcharge, so it's only for incomes below ₹50L. Tell me if the numbers look off for your case.

## 2. Quora — "How do I calculate my in-hand salary from CTC in India?"
Short answer: in-hand ≈ (gross − income tax − employee PF − professional tax) ÷ 12. Gross is not CTC: remove employer PF, gratuity and insurance first. Then tax depends on the regime you choose.
